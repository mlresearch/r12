abstract: We introduce a new convex formulation for stable principal component pursuit
  (SPCP) to decompose noisy signals into low-rank and sparse representations. For
  numerical solutions of our SPCP formulation, we first develop a convex variational
  framework and then accelerate it with quasi-Newton methods. We show, via synthetic
  and real data experiments, that our approach offers advantages over the classical
  SPCP formulations in scalability and practical parameter selection.
