abstract: Gaussian processes (GP) are a powerful tool for nonparametric regression;
  unfortunately, calcu- lating the posterior variance in a standard GP model requires
  time O(n2) in the size of the training set. Previous work by Shen et al. (2006)
  used a k-d tree structure to approximate the pos- terior mean in certain GP models.
  We extend this approach to achieve efficient approximation of the posterior covariance
  using a tree clustering on pairs of training points, and demonstrate sig- nificant
  improvements in performance with neg- ligible loss of accuracy.
