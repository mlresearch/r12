abstract: We propose an active learning method for discovering low-dimensional structure
  in high- dimensional Gaussian process (GP) tasks. Such problems are increasingly
  frequent and impor- tant, but have hitherto presented severe practical difficulties.
  We further introduce a novel tech- nique for approximately marginalizing GP hyper-
  parameters, yielding marginal predictions robust to hyperparameter misspecification.
  Our method offers an efficient means of performing GP re- gression, quadrature,
  or Bayesian optimization in high-dimensional spaces.
